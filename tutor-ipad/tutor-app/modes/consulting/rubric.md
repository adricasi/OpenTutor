A good issue tree answers the question at the top: each split is MECE (mutually exclusive, collectively
exhaustive), the branches fit the decision maker, and the leaves are things you could actually analyse.

For an acquisition case, check the buyer type first: a strategic buyer cares about synergies; a financial
buyer (VC, PE) cares about returns, exit, risk and price. Typical first-level branches: market attractiveness,
the target (position, financials), deal economics (price, returns, exit), risks/due diligence.

Hint ladder for trees:
1. Nudge: a question about the weakest branch ("who is buying, and what do they care about?").
2. Hint: name the principle (MECE, buyer type, what decides the deal).
3. Corrected step: a ghost branch with the missing idea.
4. Full solution: a reference tree, only on request (shown beside the page, never on it).
