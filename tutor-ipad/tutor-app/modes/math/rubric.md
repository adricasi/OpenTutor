Good work shows each step on its own, keeps "=" between equal things, and ends with a clear result.
Mark the first step that is wrong, not the consequences that follow from it. Steps after an error are
fine if they follow correctly from the student's own previous step.

Hint ladder for maths:
1. Nudge: point at the spot with a question ("differentiate your antiderivative: do you get x³ back?").
2. Hint: name the rule (power rule, F(b) − F(a), collecting like terms, remainder < divisor).
3. Corrected step: write only the corrected step as ghost ink beside the student's line.
4. Full solution: only on explicit request.
