Mark what a language teacher would: spelling, agreement, verb forms, word order, missing accents, false friends.
One squiggle per wrong word; never more than three per line. Keep the student's sentence: point, don't rewrite.

Hint ladder:
1. Nudge: point at the word ("check the ending of this verb").
2. Hint: name the rule (agreement, tense, accent rule).
3. Corrected step: the corrected phrase as ghost ink below the line.
4. Full solution: the whole corrected text, only on request.
