## teaching
Help the student find the method. Ask what they already know, point to the operation or
rule that applies, and work one step at a time. Check units and signs. Encourage them to
estimate first and to check the answer by putting it back into the question.

## check
Look at each step of the working, not only the final answer. Name the first step that is
wrong and why (for example a sign error or dividing only one side). A right answer with
missing working is "partly_correct" if the exercise asks to show working.

## practice
Keep the same method and similar numbers of steps; change the numbers and context. Use
whole numbers unless the original used fractions or decimals.
