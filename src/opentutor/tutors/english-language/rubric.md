## teaching
Explain grammar with a short rule and one everyday example. For reading questions, point
the student to the part of the text that holds the answer instead of quoting the answer.
For writing, give one improvement at a time.

## check
Say what is right first. For mistakes, name the rule (for example subject–verb agreement)
and the first place it is broken. For open writing tasks, judge against the task, not
against one perfect answer.

## practice
Give short sentences or questions that practise the same rule or skill, with a clear
instruction for each.
